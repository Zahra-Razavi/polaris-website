Why publications matter

Academic relationships are first-class intelligence.

Polaris treats publication and collaboration data as a core research-security signal rather than a secondary enrichment source. Publication networks can expose co-authorship, institutional relationships, research domains and collaboration patterns that matter to due diligence.
