Loupe

Investigate the open web with traceable evidence.

Loupe performs multilingual open-web investigations, resolves entities across languages, summarizes findings and links material claims back to their supporting sources.

Traceable summaries

Protected source validation

Open sources without exposing the analyst’s own IP.

When an analyst wants to validate a source, the webpage can be opened inside Polaris through a Polaris-controlled browser session. The destination website receives the session's Polaris-side network identity rather than a direct visit from the analyst’s device.
