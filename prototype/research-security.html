Research Security

OSINT workflows built around research-security decisions.

Polaris connects academic, corporate, regulatory and open-web intelligence to support due diligence on researchers, collaborators, organizations and research relationships.

Core workflows

Research-security work, not generic OSINT.

Each workflow combines Atlas relationship intelligence with Loupe open-web investigation when broader context is required.

Researcher due diligence

Review identity, publications, affiliations, corporate relationships, regulatory indicators and open-web context.

Research partnership review

Investigate universities, companies, collaborators, ownership relationships and sensitive-technology context.

Conflict-of-interest investigation

Use graph exploration and Closest Path to identify potentially relevant direct and indirect relationships.

Affiliations of concern

Examine direct and indirect institutional, research and corporate relationships in context.

Sensitive technology review

Connect research areas, publications, organizations and relevant sensitive or dual-use technology categories.

Ongoing monitoring

Continue watching selected researchers and organizations as information in Polaris changes.

Identify

Start with the person, institution, collaborator or organization under review.

Contextualize

Review publications, affiliations, corporate data, sanctions, NROs and sensitive technology information.

Connect

Use network relationships and path finding to understand indirect connections.

Investigate

Expand into multilingual open-web sources when structured data needs context.

Document

Review the evidence and source trail supporting findings.
