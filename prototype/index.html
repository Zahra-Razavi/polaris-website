Loupe source validation

Verify evidence without exposing the analyst.

Loupe links summary findings to their supporting sources. Analysts can open those webpages inside Polaris through a Polaris-controlled browser session, reducing exposure of the analyst’s own device and IP address to the destination website.

Traceability and operational privacy are presented as part of the same workflow: find the claim, inspect the evidence, validate the source.
